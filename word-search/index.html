Privacy Policy — ABC Word Search

Last updated: 31 October 2026

ABC Word Search (“the app”) is a game for learning Spanish words, developed by Nataliya Romanenko.

The app does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It works offline.

Your settings (languages, level, difficulty, table colour, sound, timer) and your game statistics (games played, days in a row, best times) are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Error reports are optional. If you tap “Report an error” and then “Send”, the text you wrote, the screenshot you chose to attach (if any), the app screen, the interface language, the device system and the app version are sent to the developer by email (through Google Apps Script, Google’s service in the developer’s own Google account). Your name, email address or any other contact details are not requested or sent. If you attach a screenshot, the app asks for access to your photos only to let you pick that one image. We use these reports only to fix errors and delete them when they are no longer needed. If you want a reply, write to the address below from your own email.

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
