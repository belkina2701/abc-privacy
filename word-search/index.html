Privacy Policy — ABC Word Search

Last updated: 27 October 2026

ABC Word Search (“the app”) is a game for learning Spanish words, developed by Nataliya Romanenko.

The app does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It works offline.

Your settings (languages, level, difficulty, table colour, sound, timer) and your game statistics (games played, days in a row, best times) are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Error reports are optional. If you tap “Report an error”, the app opens your own email app with a prepared message: your text, the app screen, the interface language and the device system. You decide whether to send it. If you choose to attach a screenshot, the app asks for access to your photos only to let you pick that one image. We use these emails only to fix errors and reply to you.

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
